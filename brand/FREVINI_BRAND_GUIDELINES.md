# Frevini Studio — Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · September 2026 · Canonical, machine-readable edition**

Companion files: `frevini-design-tokens.json` (values), `Frevini-Studio-Brand-Guidelines.pdf` (visual edition), `assets/` (fonts, logo, icons, reference imagery).

This is not a rebrand. It codifies the identity that already exists at [frevini.co.nz](https://frevini.co.nz) and extends it into a governed system for documents, presentations, posters and collateral. Anyone — a designer, an architect, a sales team, a Word or PowerPoint user, or an automated document generator — should be able to produce material from this document that is unmistakably Frevini.

Provenance labels used throughout: **A** exact (from code or official asset) · **B** observed (measured consistently in Frevini material) · **C** proposed (extension for document governance). See §10.

---

## 0. The system in one paragraph

One dark green does all the work. One editorial serif (Marcellus) carries every heading, one contemporary sans (DM Sans) carries everything else, and neither is ever bold. Pages are white or warm white, square-cornered, left-aligned and at least 40 percent empty. Photography of oak, timber and finished interiors supplies all the warmth and colour; the palette itself stays narrow. The only graphic devices are a fine green rule, a plain arrow and a thin-line icon. If a page needs a box, a gradient, a shadow or a second accent colour to work, it is not yet a Frevini page.

## 1. Brand character

### 1.1 Established by Frevini (A/B)

- **"Built not Branded."** Home-page hero statement. Substance over surface; the product proves itself.
- **"Where design intent meets technical mastery."** Home-page statement. Design and engineering are presented as one discipline, not two audiences.
- **"Better. Bespoke. European Made."** Our Story. Short, declarative, three-beat.
- **European manufacture, New Zealand studio.** Founded by Jordan Dallimore and Sean Duff; factory in Šiauliai, Lithuania; showroom at 108 Hutt Road, Kaiwharawhara, Wellington.
- **Specifier-facing.** The site speaks to architects, designers and installers ("a collaborative partner for specifiers"), supported by downloadable datasheets, installation and maintenance guides.

### 1.2 Inferred from repeated visual patterns (B)

| Characteristic | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Architectural** | Full-bleed photography, hard rectangular geometry, no rounded corners anywhere, long horizontal rules, oblique floor perspectives. |
| **Restrained** | A single brand colour used for text, buttons, footer and menu; icons in one muted tone; no accent colours; no gradients or shadows in content. |
| **Editorial** | Large serif headlines against small sans body; wide margins; text set in one narrow column beside a wide image; sentence-case headings. |
| **Tactile / material** | Every hero image is a material: grain, bark, leaf, stacked boards, the 60-colour swatch grid. |
| **Technical / credible** | Datasheet values (15/4 mm, 20/6 mm, 0.7 mm micro-bevel, WBP birch ply), 25-year warranty, FSC sourcing, PDF file sizes shown on links. |
| **Confident and warm** | Short assured headlines; warm white fields; timber tones; nothing shouts. |

### 1.3 Proposed extensions for documents (C)

- **Calm density.** A page's information load is set by whitespace first and content second.
- **Serif as signature.** Marcellus appears at least once on every page or slide, even a technical one, so the identity survives without the logo.
- **One accent per page.** Green is the accent; photography is the colour. Nothing else competes.

## 2. Logo

### 2.1 The mark (B)

The Frevini Studio logo is a wordmark: **FREVINI** in a wide geometric capitals inside a rectangular frame, with **S T U D I O** letter-spaced across the frame's lower edge and a short diagonal **tail** breaking out of the bottom-left corner. The tail gives the frame the character of a speech bubble — the studio in conversation — and is the mark's most identifiable element.

Measured proportions (from site captures): frame 270 × 95 units, stroke 5 units; FREVINI cap height 33 units, stroke 5 units; STUDIO cap height 11 units, stroke 2 units; tail descends 45° for 12 units. Overall aspect ratio ≈ 2.57 : 1 including the tail.

### 2.2 Files (see `assets/logo/`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `frevini-studio-logo-green.svg` | Primary. On white and warm white. |
| `frevini-studio-logo-white.svg` | Reversed. On Frevini Green or on the dark region of a photograph. |
| `frevini-studio-logo-black.svg` | Single-colour reproduction only (engraving, stamps, one-colour print). |
| `ref-logo-*.png` | Native-resolution crops from the site for verification. |

> **Provenance note.** The site's master SVG was not reachable from the authoring environment. The SVGs above are a vector reconstruction at the measured proportions. Replace them with the official artwork when it is supplied, keeping the same filenames.

### 2.3 Rules (C, proportions B)

- **Clear space:** the height of the tail (≈ 12 percent of the logo width) on all four sides. Nothing — text, rule, page edge, image edge — enters this zone.
- **Minimum width:** 28 mm in print; 120 px on screen. Below this, use the text form "FREVINI STUDIO" in DM Sans Medium, uppercase, 0.14em tracking.
- **Recommended width:** 42 mm on an A4 cover; 200 px on a 1920 px slide; 12 percent of the short edge on posters; 135 px on the web header.
- **Position:** top-left at the margin. Bottom-left is the only alternative (posters, closing pages).
- **Where it appears:** covers, title slides, closing/contact pages, posters. It does **not** appear on interior pages or content slides; those carry the running footer "Frevini Studio · Document title" instead. One logo per document face.
- **Backgrounds:** white, warm white, Frevini Green, or photography darkened with the standard overlay (§3.6) so that the white mark reads clearly.
- **Prohibited:** recolouring; outlines, shadows, gradients; stretching or rotating; removing STUDIO; placing a tagline inside the frame; using the wordmark as a watermark; more than one logo per page.

### 3.2 Hierarchy of use

1. **Brand primary** — every document uses all three. Green is the only colour that may carry text.
2. **Secondary** — Olive appears at most as one element type per page (the icons, or the badges, or the second chart series).
3. **Functional** — only in tables, charts and metadata. Never as decoration.
4. **Timber neutrals** — only when data or a diagram needs more than two series. Photography supplies timber colour everywhere else.

### 3.4 Proportion (C)

On a typical white page: ≥ 60 percent white or warm white, ≤ 15 percent Frevini Green (text, rules, one field), the remainder photography. A green page (closing, section divider) reverses this. No page carries more than one solid green field.

### 3.5 Where the site breaks its own rule (B, resolved C)

The world map on Our Story is pure black; the cookie bar is black. In documents, black is reserved for photographic overlays; diagrams are drawn in Frevini Green.

### 3.6 Photographic overlay (B)

Black at 30–45 percent opacity over imagery that will carry white text (observed at ≈ 40 percent). Alternatively a bottom-up gradient from black 55 percent to 0 percent over the lower half. No coloured overlays; no green tints on photography.

## 4. Typography

### 4.1 Typefaces

| Role | Typeface | Weights used | Source | Provenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / editorial | **Marcellus** (Regular) | 400 only | Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1 · `assets/fonts/marcellus/` | B — identified by rendered comparison of "Built not Branded", "Our Story" and "Where design intent…" against the captures; exact match of the inscriptional R, the slanted t and the two-storey a and g |
| Functional / body | **DM Sans** | 300, 400, 500 (600 for key numbers ≥ 24 pt; 700 never) | Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1 · `assets/fonts/dm-sans/` | B — identified by rendered comparison of body copy; exact match of the single-storey g, straight y and the ampersand |

Marcellus is a single-weight inscriptional roman: low contrast, wide proportions, no italic. It carries every heading, the navigation, quotations and key numbers. DM Sans is the working typeface: body, sub-headings, labels, tables, captions, links and UI. Sub-headings on the site are DM Sans **Regular**, larger rather than bolder; documents keep this: hierarchy comes from size, family and space, not weight.

The site's "BOOK CONSULTATION" button is set in the browser's default sans (an inconsistency). Documents and digital collateral set buttons in DM Sans Medium (§7.4).

### 4.2 Fallback system (C)

Chosen for visual compatibility, not convenience.

- **Marcellus →** Palatino Linotype (Windows) / Book Antiqua / Palatino (macOS) → Georgia. All are humanist romans of similar proportion and modest contrast. Never Times New Roman, Cambria or a bold serif.
- **DM Sans →** Aptos (Office 2023+) → Segoe UI → Helvetica Neue → Arial. Never Calibri (too narrow, too soft).

CSS stacks: `"Marcellus", "Palatino Linotype", "Book Antiqua", Palatino, Georgia, serif` and `"DM Sans", Aptos, "Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`.

### 4.4 Setting rules (C)

- Left-aligned, ragged right. Centre only a single-line statement on an image-led cover.
- Measure: 55–75 characters. Body never wider than 8 of 12 columns on A4 (≈ 112 mm).
- No bold in running text. Emphasis is a new sentence, a pull quote or a key number.
- All caps only for eyebrow, table headers, buttons and metadata — always tracked.
- Marcellus is never italicised, condensed, outlined or set in caps below 12 pt.
- Hyphenation off. No widows or orphans; keep at least two lines together.
- Headings are sentence case with a full stop only when the heading is a statement ("Built not Branded." / "Why oak?").
- Numerals: lining figures; thin space or none in "15/4 mm", "0.7 mm", "1750–2400 mm"; en-dash for ranges.

## 5. Grid and spacing

### 5.1 Spacing scale (C, proportions B)

One scale across media. **1 unit ≈ 4 mm print / 8 px web (1366) / 16 px slide (1920).**

| Token | Print | Web | Slide | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XS | 2 mm | 4 px | 8 px | Rule offset below CTA baseline; icon-to-label gap |
| S | 4 mm | 8 px | 16 px | Paragraph gap; image-to-caption; table cell vertical padding |
| M | 8 mm | 16 px | 32 px | Space after heading; table cell horizontal padding |
| L | 12 mm | 24 px | 48 px | Callout padding; column gap between text and image |
| XL | 24 mm | 48 px | 96 px | Space before heading; between blocks |
| XXL | 40 mm | 80 px | 160 px | Between sections; top of a section opener |

Whitespace is not what is left over. **At least 40 percent of any page or slide is empty field.** The top 22 mm of an A4 page (top 72 px of a slide) carries at most an eyebrow.

### 5.2 A4 portrait (C)

- Page 210 × 297 mm, bleed 3 mm.
- Margins: top 22, right 20, bottom 24, left 20 mm → content area 170 × 251 mm.
- Grid: 12 columns, 4 mm gutters, 10.5 mm columns.
- Common splits: text only 8/12 (max measure); text + image 5 + 7 or 7 + 5; sidebar 3 + 9; two-column text 6 + 6.
- Running footer, baseline 12 mm from the bottom edge: left "Document title · Frevini Studio", right page number, both in Footer style. No header; technical documents may carry a document code top-right in Eyebrow style.
- Images sit on column edges or bleed. Never inset with a border.

### 5.3 A4 landscape (C)

297 × 210 mm; 20 mm margins; 12 columns, 4 mm gutters. Same footer. Used for boards and wide tables only.

### 5.4 Presentations 16:9 (C)

Reference canvas 1920 × 1080 px (13.33 × 7.5 in).

- Safe margins: top 72, bottom 72, left 96, right 96 px.
- Grid: 12 columns, 24 px gutters, 122 px columns.
- Eyebrow baseline at y = 132. Title block y = 160–320. Body area y = 372–940. Footer baseline y = 1016.
- Image zone: full bleed, or columns 7–12 from top to bottom edge.
- Content slides carry a footer — left "FREVINI STUDIO · Document title" in Eyebrow style, right slide number — and no logo. Logo (200 px wide, top-left) appears on the title and closing slides only.
- One idea per slide. Body text never below 16 pt. Never more than 60 words on a slide.

### 5.5 Posters and one-pagers (C)

- **Posters (A3–A0):** margins 5 percent of the short edge; 6-column grid with 1.5 percent gutters; logo 12 percent of the short edge, top-left or bottom-left; headline in the upper third, left-aligned; image ≥ 55 percent of the page when image-led; at most three information zones (statement, image, contact/CTA).
- **One-pagers (A4):** top 120 mm is a hero zone (image or statement); two or three equal columns beneath; a single text CTA bottom-left; the footer carries contact details in Footer style.

## 6. Document design language

### 6.1 Cover archetypes (C)

**1. Image-led.** Full-bleed photograph (architecture, material or detail) with the standard overlay. White logo top-left at 42 mm. Title block bottom-left, aligned to the margin: Eyebrow (document type) → Display title (max two lines) → metadata line (version · month year). Nothing else. Use for proposals, capability documents, lookbooks.

**2. Editorial.** Warm white or white field. Green logo top-left at 42 mm. Title block starts at 40 percent of page height: Eyebrow → Display title → one Body-large sentence. Metadata line in Eyebrow style beneath. Optional single image: either columns 7–12 of the lower half, or a full-width band no taller than 80 mm bleeding off the foot (the cover of the visual guide uses this form), or no image at all. Use for reports, journals, guidelines, presentations to print.

**3. Technical.** White field. Green logo top-left at 36 mm. Document code top-right in Eyebrow style. H1 title at 25 percent of page height, followed by a key-value block (Product · Collection · Revision · Date) set as a two-column table with 0.35 pt rules. No imagery, or one product image in columns 7–12. Use for datasheets, specifications, compliance material, installation and maintenance guides.

### 6.2 Internal page types (C)

| Page | Anatomy |
|---|---|
| **Section opener** | Eyebrow with section number, H1 at 30 percent page height, one Body-large paragraph, the rest of the page empty or a single image bleeding off the lower edge. |
| **Standard text page** | Eyebrow, H2, body in columns 1–8. Right-hand columns 9–12 empty or holding a caption, a key number or a technical note. |
| **Text + image** | 5 + 7 or 7 + 5. Image on column edges or bleeding to the outer edge; text column starts on the same baseline as the image's top edge. Caption in Caption style beneath the image, left-aligned to its edge. |
| **Full image** | Bleed on all sides. Optional caption in white over the overlay, bottom-left at the margin. No other text. |
| **Technical / data** | H2, then tables, specification lists and diagrams. Tables span 8 or 12 columns. Technical notes in columns 9–12 or beneath. Generous vertical spacing (XL between blocks). |
| **Quote / statement** | Warm white field. Quotation style in columns 2–10, attribution beneath in Caption. Nothing else. |
| **Closing / contact** | Frevini Green field. White logo top-left. H1 in white ("Let's talk timber."). Contact block bottom-left: phone, email, address in Body style. Optional single CTA. |

### 6.3 Presentation slide archetypes (C)

| Slide | Anatomy |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Image-led (full-bleed + overlay) or green field. White logo top-left. Eyebrow → Display title → metadata, bottom-left. |
| **Section divider** | Warm white field. Section number in Eyebrow, H1 in the title block, nothing else. Or green field with white type. |
| **Statement** | One Marcellus sentence (Display or Quotation size), columns 1–9, vertically centred. Optional image in columns 10–12 bleeding right. |
| **Text** | Eyebrow, H2, body in columns 1–7 (max 60 words). Columns 8–12 empty. |
| **Text + image** | Text columns 1–5; image columns 7–12 bleeding top, right and bottom. |
| **Full image** | Bleed. Caption bottom-left in white if needed. |
| **Comparison / data** | H2 plus one table or one chart spanning columns 1–12, or two key numbers side by side (Key number + Caption). |
| **Closing / contact** | Green field. White logo, H1, contact block. Slide number omitted. |

Not permitted: title bars, coloured header bands, bullet-heavy slides, more than one chart per slide, clip art, rounded cards, drop shadows, slide transitions other than none/fade.

### 6.4 Posters and one-pagers (C)

Hero statement first: one Marcellus line at ≥ 8 percent of the short edge. The image dominates — ≥ 55 percent of the area — and the statement sits either over the overlay or in the field beneath. Logo at 12 percent of the short edge. One CTA with rule; one contact line. Minimum information principle: if a passer-by needs more than five seconds, it belongs in a document, not a poster.

## 7. Graphic language

### 7.1 Rules and lines (B weights, C applications)

The site's recurring device is a fine horizontal rule beneath a text link, spanning exactly the label and its arrow. This is the only decorative line in the system.

| Rule | Weight | Colour | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTA rule | 2 px web / 1 pt print | Frevini Green (white on green or imagery) | Width of the label including the arrow; sits XS below the baseline |
| Section rule | 1 px / 0.5 pt | Green 20 | Full text measure |
| Table header rule | 0.75 pt | Frevini Green | Table width |
| Table row rule | 0.35 pt | Green 20 | Table width |
| Logo frame | 5 units of 270 | as logo | — |

Rules are horizontal only. No vertical rules, no boxes, no borders on images or cards. The only framed element in the system is the logo.

### 7.2 Arrows (B)

A plain right arrow (→, or `icons/arrow-right`) follows a link label after one space: "Book a consultation →". Its stroke matches the text; it is never bold, never doubled, never used as a bullet and never appears in headings. On the web it may animate 4 px to the right on hover; nothing else moves.

### 7.3 Icons (B stroke character, C rules)

Thin-line, single-colour, rounded corners, no fill. Drawn on a 24-unit grid with a 1.5-unit stroke (2 px at 64 px). Colour: Olive on warm white, Frevini Green on white, White on green. Size 48–64 px on screen, 12–16 mm in print, with S gap to the label beneath. At most four per page, used only to label categories of resource or contact channels. Never as bullets, never filled, never multicoloured, never inside circles. The set in `assets/icons/` is drawn to this specification.

### 7.4 Buttons (B, digital only)

Filled Frevini Green rectangle, no radius, 48 px tall, 32 px horizontal padding, DM Sans Medium 14 px uppercase +0.06em in white. One primary button per screen. Secondary actions use the text CTA with rule. There are no outlined buttons and no buttons in print.

### 7.5 Shapes (B)

None. Frevini has no supporting shapes, patterns, gradients, blobs, swooshes or textures. The circular count badge on "Your order" is a UI element, not a brand device. Restraint is the device.

## 8. Photography

### 8.1 Categories (C, evidence B)

| Category | Content | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Architecture** | Finished interiors where the floor contributes materially: long views, oblique floor angles, natural light across boards, furniture as scale. | Covers, section openers, full-image pages |
| **Detail** | Grain, texture, board junctions, bevels, profiles, edges, brushing and sanding marks. | Text + image pages, quotes, closing pages |
| **Material / process** | Oak forest and leaf, logs and bark, stacked boards, finishing lines, sample swatches and the 60-colour grid. | Our Story-type narratives, technical documents |
| **Human** | People only when working with the material or occupying the space naturally: an installer, an architect with samples. Never posed, never looking at camera. | Sparingly, in journals and proposals |

### 8.2 Principles

- **Light:** natural, directional, soft. Late-afternoon warmth is the reference. No studio flash look, no HDR.
- **Colour:** warm-neutral white balance; restrained saturation; greens and browns from the subject, not from grading. No filters, no vignettes.
- **Composition:** level horizontals; oblique views of floors are encouraged (the herringbone hero); keep at least one edge of the frame quiet so type can sit there.
- **Cropping:** square corners; full bleed or aligned to grid columns; no insets with borders; no circular or rounded masks.
- **Text on imagery:** white only, over a naturally deep region or the standard overlay (black 30–45 percent). Never over faces, grain detail or the horizon.
- **Scale:** one hero image per page at most; support images at column width; never a collage.
- **Resolution:** 300 ppi at reproduction size for print; 2× for screen.

### 8.3 Do not use

Generic stock lifestyle photography; renders that pretend to be photographs; isolated product cut-outs on pure white except in sample grids; heavy contrast or HDR; coloured overlays; cluttered showrooms; competitors' or unidentified floors; images with visible logos or watermarks; drone or wide-angle distortion.

## 9. Voice and copy

Frevini writes the way it builds: short, exact, unhurried. The site pairs a two-to-five-word statement with a sentence of technical proof.

### 9.1 Principles (B/C)

1. **Say it short.** Headlines are three to six words: "Built not Branded." "Why oak?" "Better. Bespoke. European Made." Full stops are allowed in headlines when the line is a statement.
2. **Specific beats superlative.** Numbers, materials and processes do the persuading: 25-year residential warranty, 15/4 mm, FSC-certified, WBP birch plywood, Šiauliai. Never "premium quality" or "world-class".
3. **Design and technical in one breath.** Each paragraph pairs intent with proof: "hardwearing, durable and full of natural character, with a distinctive grain that helps everyday marks blend into the floor."
4. **Assured, not salesy.** No exclamation marks, no urgency, no "don't miss out". Invitations are calm: "Connect with us to explore how we can support your next project."
5. **Plain New Zealand English, European precision.** Sentence case, first person plural ("we use", "we control"), metric units, en-dashes for ranges, no jargon that an architect would not use.
6. **Address the specifier as a peer.** "A collaborative partner for specifiers." Not "customers", not "clients" in headline copy.

### 9.2 Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Engineered oak. Made in Lithuania. Specified in New Zealand." | "The ultimate premium flooring solution for discerning homeowners!" |
| "Total thickness 15/4 mm or 20/6 mm. Oak lamella 4 mm or 6 mm." | "Super-thick, ultra-durable boards." |
| "Book a consultation →" | "CLICK HERE TO GET STARTED NOW" |
| "UV oil: a soft matte surface that can be refreshed as the floor ages." | "Our revolutionary finish technology transforms your space." |
| "Let's talk timber." | "Contact our friendly sales team today!" |

## 10. Information design

### 10.1 Tables (C)

- Header row: Table-header style, Frevini Green text, no fill on white pages; Warm White fill permitted on white pages for long tables.
- Rules: 0.75 pt green beneath the header; 0.35 pt Green 20 between rows; none above the header, none vertical, none around the table.
- Cell padding: S vertical, M horizontal. Numbers right-aligned; text left-aligned; units in the header, not the cells.
- Alternate-row fill (Green 8) only for tables over twelve rows.
- Never: Office default styles, coloured header bands, bold cell text, centred columns, merged decorative cells.

### 10.2 Charts (C)

- Series colours in order: Frevini Green, Olive, Oak Natural, Oak Fumed. Maximum four series; usually two.
- Bars are flat rectangles with no outline; lines are 1.5 pt; no markers unless data points are sparse; no 3D, no gradients, no shadows.
- Axes in Green 60 at 0.5 pt; horizontal gridlines only, Green 20 at 0.35 pt; label values directly on bars where possible and drop the axis.
- Chart title in H3; source or note in Technical style beneath.
- Pie charts are avoided; use a bar or a key number.

### 10.3 Diagrams (C)

Line drawings in Frevini Green at 0.75 pt on white or warm white; labels in Technical style; no fills except Timber neutrals for material layers (e.g. a board cross-section: Oak Natural lamella over Oak Pale plywood). Arrowheads are open, 45°.

### 10.4 Specifications (C)

Two-column key–value list: keys in Table-header style (uppercase, tracked), values in Table style; 0.35 pt Green 20 rules between rows; keys occupy 4 of 12 columns.

### 10.5 Callouts (C)

A Warm White field with L padding and no border. Optional eyebrow ("Note", "Specification tip"). Body style. One per page. On a warm white page, the callout is white.

### 10.6 Key numbers (C)

Marcellus at 36 pt (A4) or 60 pt (slide) with a Caption-style label beneath, left-aligned; up to three in a row, separated by XL. Example: **25** / Year residential warranty · **60** / Colours across three collections · **8 %** / Target moisture content ± 2 %.

### 10.7 Quotations, captions, footnotes, references (C)

- Quotation: Quotation style, columns 2–10, no quotation marks larger than the text, attribution in Caption beneath with an en-dash.
- Captions: Caption style, beneath the image, left-aligned to its edge, S below.
- Footnotes: superscript numerals in the text; notes in Technical style at the foot of the content area above the footer, separated by a 0.5 pt Green 20 rule 24 mm long.
- References: Technical style, hanging indent, numbered.
- Technical notes: eyebrow "Note" followed by Body on the same column; no box.

### 10.8 Document metadata and revisions (C)

- Cover metadata block, bottom-left, in Eyebrow style: DOCUMENT TYPE · VERSION 1.0 · SEPTEMBER 2026.
- File naming: `Frevini-<DocumentType>-<Subject>-v<major>.<minor>.pdf`.
- Revision table (technical documents, inside cover or last page): Version · Date · Change · Issued by, in Table style.
- PDF: fonts embedded; sRGB for screen, FOGRA39 CMYK for press; 3 mm bleed with crop marks for print.

## 11. Governance — the Frevini Test

Before a document, deck or poster is issued, answer each question honestly. Any "no" sends it back.

1. **Is the typography unmistakably Frevini?** Marcellus for every heading, DM Sans for everything else, nothing bold.
2. **Is there enough whitespace?** At least 40 percent of the page empty; nothing in the top 22 mm but an eyebrow.
3. **Is the colour palette restrained?** Green, warm white, white — and Olive at most once. No other hue outside photography.
4. **Is the imagery doing useful work?** Each image shows material, detail or a finished space, and there is at most one hero per page.
5. **Is the information hierarchy immediately clear?** One eyebrow, one heading, one idea; tables with horizontal rules only.
6. **Have unnecessary decorative elements been removed?** No boxes, shadows, gradients, rounded corners, icons as bullets.
7. **Does it balance design confidence with technical credibility?** A statement and a proof on every spread.
8. **Would it look at home beside the Frevini website?** Hide the logo. Is it still Frevini?

### Issue checklist

- [ ] Fonts embedded (Marcellus, DM Sans) or the fallback stack applied consistently
- [ ] Logo: correct variant, clear space respected, one per document face, ≥ 28 mm / 120 px
- [ ] Colours match the token values exactly (`#253C1B`, `#FAF5EB`, `#8B9770`)
- [ ] Grid margins and footer per §5; page numbers present
- [ ] Body measure ≤ 8 columns; no text below 7 pt in print / 16 pt on slides (captions excepted)
- [ ] Tables: no vertical rules; header rule 0.75 pt; row rules 0.35 pt
- [ ] Charts: ≤ 4 series in the approved order; no gridlines but light horizontals
- [ ] Images: 300 ppi at size; overlay 30–45 percent where text sits on imagery
- [ ] Copy: sentence case, no exclamation marks, numbers where claims are made
- [ ] Metadata block and file name follow §10.8
