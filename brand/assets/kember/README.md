## Using the lock-up

Umber on white or Oak White; white on Kember Umber or darkened photography; black for single-colour reproduction only. Minimum width 30 mm print / 130 px screen (wordmark alone 22 mm / 96 px). Clear space = half the wordmark height on all sides. Top-left at the margin; covers, title and closing slides, posters and product sheets only. Never grey, never blue, never with "kreative interiors".
