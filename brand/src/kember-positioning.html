01 — Summary

The product has a name.
The brand needs a decision.

The New Zealand flooring product is PrimeOak® Flooring, Signature Collection: An Cuong’s registered product, in American Northern red oak from Michigan, designed and engineered in the United States. What decides the collateral is the relationship between Kember and An Cuong, which no document we hold sets out.

  1. 1  PrimeOak and Kember’s E3plank share the same 4 mm face, 11 mm core, cut names and six colour names. They are very probably one product family under two brands. Kember has not confirmed it.
  2. 2  Three brand architectures are possible. The recommendation is Kember Interiors as the master brand, carrying PrimeOak® as the named product, with An Cuong named only where certification and warranty require it.
  3. 3  The positioning territory with the most room is one oak for the whole interior: floor, stair, wall, ceiling and joinery, colour-matched from one range. The catalogue supports it today.
  4. 4  The product architecture that follows adds Stair + Components as a first-class category and names a curated colour selection to be chosen from twelve.
  5. 5  Eight decisions are needed from Courtney and Kember before Brand Guidelines V1.2 and any collateral. The largest are the brand relationship, the warranty and installer model, and the launch selection.

04 — Brand relationship

How the names appear on a page.

The question is not what Kember Interiors is called. It is how PrimeOak®, An Cuong, the Masterpiece Series and Kember Interiors appear on a New Zealand product sheet. Three architectures are possible.

Option A
Manufacturer brand, distributed

An Cuong PrimeOak® is the product brand; Kember Interiors is the New Zealand distributor. Collateral leads with PrimeOak and the Masterpiece Series; Kember appears as “distributed in New Zealand by”.

For. Honest to the mark; warranty and certifications attach to An Cuong; the catalogue can be reused.

Against. Kember Interiors becomes a footer logo, which defeats the brand system; An Cuong has no New Zealand recognition; the Masterpiece register and the catalogue’s design language are not the Kember system.

Option B
Kember-branded product

Kember Interiors owns the presentation; the flooring is “Kember [name] flooring, with PrimeOak® Core” or similar. An Cuong is named only on certificates and the warranty.

For. Strongest expression of the brand system; New Zealand-facing from the first page.

Against. Needs a licence to present An Cuong’s registered product under Kember’s name, which may not exist; risks the 15-year warranty if partner status is tied to An Cuong’s branding; creates a third name for one product.

Option C · recommended
Kember master brand, PrimeOak® named

Kember Interiors is the brand on every face. The flooring is named PrimeOak® Flooring as a product within Kember Interiors, with an attribution line in the technical block. An Cuong appears where certification, warranty or the mark require it, in technical style, never in the title block. Masterpiece Series is not used in New Zealand.

For. Uses the real name and its certifications without ceding the page; one name across Kember’s and An Cuong’s documents; needs a distribution and trademark-use agreement, not a rebrand licence; codes and datasheet values lift unchanged.

Against. Two names on the page need a fixed hierarchy; PrimeOak is a name Kember does not own and could lose.

Conditions for Option C, all needing written confirmation from Kember
  1. 1  Kember Interiors may use the PrimeOak® mark in New Zealand collateral.
  2. 2  Whether Kember Interiors, or an installer network it accredits, can hold An Cuong Authorized Partner status so the 15 / 5-year warranty applies.
  3. 3  Whether “Masterpiece Series” must appear anywhere. Recommendation: no.
  4. 4  The shipped specification for New Zealand: finish, thicknesses, formats, packs.

05 — Positioning

Three territories. One with room.

Tested against five criteria: true today from the evidence; room for the wider Kember range; distinct from Frevini; credible to a specifier; supported by assets we have or can make.

Territory 1
Engineered American oak flooring

“American oak. Engineered for stability.” The V1.1 working line. Narrow, true, provable.

True today, yes. Room to grow, no. Distinct from Frevini, partly. Specifier credibility, high. Assets, yes.

Verdict. A strong product-level message for flooring, not a master-brand position.

Territory 2 · recommended
One oak for the whole interior

Floor, stair, wall, ceiling and joinery in one American red oak, colour-matched from one range. The interior reads as one material because it is one.

True today, yes: matched stair parts, trims and panels are in the catalogue; Kember makes wall panels, doors and cabinetry. Room to grow, the most. Distinct from Frevini, clearly. Credibility, high: coordinated material across floor, stair and joinery is a real specifier problem. Assets, partly: matched stair-and-floor photography is the gap.

Verdict. Recommended. It turns “Kember Interiors” from a company name into a proposition.

Territory 3
Precision-engineered interior systems

Kember as an engineering brand for interior components, oak being the first material.

True today, partly. Room to grow, large but abstract. Distinct, yes. Credibility, medium: a claim before it is a range. Assets, no.

Verdict. Not now. A later evolution of Territory 2 if Kember’s non-oak capability comes to New Zealand.

Draft proposition under Territory 2, for Courtney to edit

Kember Interiors brings one American red oak to the whole interior: floor, stair, wall and joinery, colour-matched from a single range and engineered as all-oak construction.

One oak. The whole interior.

Candidates to test: “American oak, floor to ceiling.” · “Floor, stair, wall. One oak.” Flooring line retained at product level: “American oak. Engineered for stability.”

Reasons to believe, all from the catalogue

American Northern red oak from within 120 km in Michigan, designed and engineered in the United States · all-oak construction, 4 mm face on a cross-directional oak core · colour-matched stair parts, trims and panels from the same twelve colours · NAF, EPA TSCA Title VI, JAS F4 Star · up to 304 × 3600 mm.

Audience

Primary: architects, interior designers and specifiers on high-value residential, multi-residential and hospitality work. Secondary: builders, installers and developers. The homeowner is reached through the specifier.

Competitive frame, to validate

New Zealand engineered oak is mostly European white oak on plywood or softwood cores. Kember’s checkable differences: red oak from a named region; an oak core; matched stair and panel components; NAF, TSCA and F4 Star, to verify against Homestar and Green Star; 304 × 3600 mm at 19 mm.

Relationship to Frevini

Frevini: European oak, editorial, studio-led. Kember: American red oak, engineered, whole-interior. Kember leads with wide plank and matched components, not herringbone and chevron.

06 — Product architecture

Brand, category, product, format.
