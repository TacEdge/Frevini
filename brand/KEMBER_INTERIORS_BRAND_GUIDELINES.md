# Kember Interiors — Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · September 2026 · Canonical, machine-readable edition**

Companion files: `kember-design-tokens.json` (values), `Kember-Interiors-Brand-Guidelines.pdf` (visual edition), `KEMBER_FREVINI_SYSTEM_MAPPING.md` (how each Frevini rule was retained, adapted or set aside), `assets/kember/` (logo, icons, reference imagery, captures). Fonts are shared with Frevini in `assets/fonts/`.

Kember Interiors is the New Zealand-facing brand for Kember's American oak interior products. Its design system is the Frevini Studio system (v1.1), applied to Kember's own name, colour, material, engineering and products. The two brands are governed to one standard and are never the same brand.

Provenance labels: **A** exact Kember · **B** existing Kember · **C** transferred from Frevini · **D** Kember adaptation. See §13.

---

## 0. The system in one paragraph

One deep umber does all the work. Marcellus sets every hierarchy and DM Sans carries everything else; neither is set bold in the standard hierarchy. Pages are white or Oak White, square-cornered, left-aligned and built on open field. Photography of American oak — the finished space, the material, the construction, the making, the application — supplies the colour the palette withholds. The graphic language is Frevini's fine rule, plain arrow and thin-line icon, plus one Kember device: the three-layer section, used only where it informs. If a page needs a box, a gradient, a rounded card or a second accent colour, it is not yet a Kember page.

## 1. Brand character

### 1.1 Established by Kember (A, as indexed from kemberfloors.com and its catalogues)

- **American Northern Oak**, "sustainably sourced from certified North American forests". Wear layer and all plies are hardwood.
- **E3plank**: balanced three-ply construction — 4 mm wear layer, 11 mm proprietary two-directional core, 4 mm balancing layer — "minimising expansion and contraction due to temperature and humidity changes"; "cross grain restrains width expansion".
- Installed like a ¾-inch solid floor, nail-down, in random lengths; "reduces minor gapping between planks during seasonal changes".
- **Hardwax oil** finish. Warranty "for the life of every floor we sell" on lamination and finish, provided documented maintenance is carried out.
- Family-owned, vertically integrated; flooring, wall panels, interior doors and cabinetry; a design team working with architects, designers, owners and contractors.

### 1.2 Observed in existing Kember communications (B)

- Rounded geometric unicase wordmark in grey with "kreative interiors" in blue; Montserrat throughout; bright blue accents; rounded image cards; circular buttons; quote-mark glyphs; photo-filled display type; blue-graded, render-like hero imagery; deep umber section fields; US promotional copy ("Transform your living spaces", "gorgeous for years").
- What survives into the New Zealand system: the wordmark letterforms and the deep umber field. What is set aside: everything else in this list.

### 1.3 Character of the New Zealand brand (C/D)

**Engineered, material, exact, calm.** Kember is the engineering-led American oak brand: the construction is the story, the oak is the colour, and the documents are specified rather than sold. Where Frevini is editorial and European, Kember is technical and North American; both are architectural, restrained and photographed the same way.

## 2. Logo

### 2.1 The mark (B geometry, D lock-up)

The core **KEMBER** wordmark is retained: six unicase letterforms of equal height, uniform stroke (≈ 10 percent of letter height), rounded e and B bowls, a full-height r with a curved top arm, and the ™ at the top right. It is not redrawn. "kreative interiors" is retired for New Zealand.

The New Zealand lock-up adds **INTERIORS** beneath in DM Sans Medium, uppercase, +0.22 em, right-aligned to the wordmark (the position the existing tagline occupies), at ≈ 19 percent of the wordmark height and ≈ 30 percent of it below the baseline. Lock-up aspect ratio ≈ 3.08 : 1.

### 2.3 Rules (C, adapted D)

- **Colour:** Kember Umber or white. Black for single-colour print only. The grey (#AEB3B7, 2.1 : 1 on white) and blue of the current mark are not used in New Zealand material.
- **Clear space:** half the wordmark height on all sides.
- **Minimum width:** lock-up 30 mm print / 130 px screen; wordmark alone 22 mm / 96 px. Below that, "KEMBER INTERIORS" in DM Sans Medium, uppercase, +0.14 em.
- **Recommended width:** 44 mm on an A4 cover; 210 px on a 1920 px slide; 12 percent of the short edge on posters; 150 px on the web.
- **Position:** top-left at the margin; bottom-left on posters and closing pages.
- **Appears on:** covers, title and closing slides, posters, product sheets. Interior pages carry the running footer "Document title · Kember Interiors" instead. One mark per document face.
- **Prohibited:** "kreative interiors"; recolouring; outlines, shadows, gradients; stretching or rotation; watermarks; the section device inside the clear space; two marks on one face.

### 3.2 Hierarchy and combinations (C)

Three colours, not eleven. Core carries every document and is what a Word or PowerPoint theme should hold; Utility appears only in tables, charts, icons and diagrams; Black only over photography. Approved pairs: umber on white or Oak White; white or Oak White on umber; white on photography with overlay; Slate on Oak White or white for graphics and large text only. Never: Slate text below 18 pt; blue anywhere in documents; umber text on photography; black text.

### 3.3 Proportion (C)

On a white page: ≥ 60 percent white or Oak White, ≤ 15 percent umber, the remainder photography. An umber page (closing, divider) reverses this. One solid umber field per page.

## 5. Grid and spacing (C — identical to Frevini)

- **Spacing:** XS 2 · S 4 · M 8 · L 12 · XL 24 · XXL 40 mm (×2 px web, ×4 px slide). Paragraph gap S; after a heading M; before a heading XL; between sections XXL; callout padding L; table cells S vertical, M horizontal.
- **Whitespace is structural.** About 40 percent open field in editorial, presentation and marketing layouts. Technical documents — datasheets, specification sheets, colour cards — may carry greater density while retaining the margins, the spacing scale, clear grouping and breathing room between blocks.
- **A4 portrait:** margins 22 / 20 / 24 / 20 mm; 12 columns, 4 mm gutters; body within 8 columns; footer baseline 12 mm from the foot: "Document title · Kember Interiors" left, page number right; technical documents carry a document code top-right in eyebrow style.
- **A4 landscape:** 20 mm margins, 12 columns; boards and wide tables only.
- **16:9:** 1920 × 1080 reference; safe margins 72 / 96 / 72 / 96 px; 12 columns, 24 px gutters; eyebrow y 132, title block y 160–320, body y 372–940, footer y 1016 ("KEMBER INTERIORS · title" left, number right); lock-up 210 px top-left on title and closing slides only; one idea per slide, body ≥ 16 pt, ≤ 60 words.
- **Posters:** margins 5 percent of the short edge; 6 columns; lock-up 12 percent of the short edge; headline upper third; image ≥ 55 percent when image-led; three zones at most.
- **One-pagers:** hero zone top 120 mm; two or three columns beneath; one CTA bottom-left.

## 6. Photography

Frevini's discipline, Kember's subjects. (C discipline, D categories.)

| Category | Content | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Architecture** | Finished spaces where the oak floor, wall or ceiling is the subject; natural light across boards; joinery as scale. | Covers, openers, full-image pages |
| **Material** | American oak grain, texture, board edges, sawn character (live, rift, rift-and-quartered). | Text + image pages, product sheets, colour cards |
| **Engineering** | The three-layer construction, cross-grain core, profiles, tongue-and-groove, components. | Engineering pages, datasheets, slides — the section device stands in where no photograph exists |
| **Making** | Factory, milling, pressing, finishing, where high-quality assets exist. | Narrative and specifier documents |
| **Application** | Floor, wall, ceiling, doors, cabinetry in use; one application per image; the material continuous across the frame. | Product and category pages |

Principles: natural, directional light; warm-neutral white balance; restrained saturation with colour from the oak, not the grade; level horizontals; one quiet edge for type; square corners; full bleed or column edges; one hero per page; support images at column width; never a collage. Text on imagery: white only, over a naturally deep region or a black overlay at 30–45 percent, bottom-left at the margin.

Do not use: generic luxury-house imagery; heavy blue grading; HDR; decorative filters; brown-tinted overlays; stock lifestyle photography; busy collages; rounded image cards; renders presented as photographs; unnecessary overlays; unidentified or competitors' floors.

**Status (D).** The imagery in `assets/kember/imagery/` is cut from captures of kemberfloors.com and is suitable for reference and column-width reproduction only. A photography commission covering the five categories, at 300 ppi for print, is required before production collateral.

## 7. Graphic language

### 7.1 Transferred unchanged (C)

- **Rules:** CTA rule 2 px / 1 pt umber, the width of the label, XS below the baseline; section rule 0.5 pt Umber 20, full measure; table header rule 0.75 pt umber; row rules 0.35 pt Umber 20. Horizontal only. No boxes, no borders on images, no vertical rules.
- **Arrow:** → after the label, one space; never bold, doubled, a bullet or in headings.
- **Icons:** thin-line, 24-unit grid, 1.5 stroke, round joins, no fill; Slate on Oak White, umber on white, white on umber; 12–16 mm; four per page at most; never as bullets. Set in `assets/kember/icons/`.
- **Button (digital only):** umber rectangle, 48 px, no radius, DM Sans Medium 14 px uppercase +0.06 em in white; one per screen. No outlined buttons; no circular buttons; none in print.
- **Shapes:** none — with one exception below.

### 7.2 The section device (D)

Kember's engineered construction is its one additional visual asset, and it is an information device first.

- **Form:** three horizontal bands in 4 : 11 : 4 proportion (wear layer, core, balancing layer). The core's grain is drawn as hairlines perpendicular to the faces (the cross-grain). Outline and layer lines 0.75 pt umber; hairlines 0.5 pt Oak Mid.
- **Fills:** Oak Light for the wear and balancing layers and Oak Pale for the core when the device explains construction; no fills when it labels a document type.
- **Labels:** Technical style, to the right, one per layer, with the dimension in millimetres.
- **Where:** engineering pages, technical datasheets, product sheets, engineering and product slides, technical covers.
- **Limits:** once per document face; at most 40 mm wide in print, 360 px on a slide; never rotated, patterned, tiled, used as a background, placed in the lock-up clear space, or coloured outside umber and the oak neutrals.

## 8. Voice and copy

Frevini's verbal discipline with Kember's facts (C principles, D examples).

1. **Say it short.** "American oak. Engineered for stability." "Three plies. All hardwood." "Floor, wall, ceiling. One oak."
2. **Specific beats superlative.** 4 mm wear layer, 11 mm two-directional core, 19 mm total, hardwax oil, lifetime lamination warranty (maintained). Never "exceptional", "superior", "industry-leading".
3. **Design and engineering in one breath.** Each paragraph pairs the space with the construction that makes it possible.
4. **Assured, not salesy.** No exclamation marks, no "transform", no "gorgeous", no "peace of mind".
5. **New Zealand English, metric first.** Sentence case, "we", millimetres with inches in brackets, en-dashes for ranges.
6. **Address the specifier as a peer.** Architects, designers and installers; "request samples", not "buy now".
7. **Say what Kember says.** Claims are stated as Kember states them. Where Kember publishes no figure — humidity range, emissions class, certification body — the document says nothing rather than something plausible. Removing promotional language never alters technical meaning.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| American oak. Engineered for stability. | Transform your living spaces with the exceptional quality of American Northern Oak! |
| Three plies, all hardwood. A 4 mm wear layer over an 11 mm two-directional core. | Superior strength and durability for modern living. |
| Installed like a 19 mm solid floor. Less seasonal gapping. | With our superior scratch and stain resistance, your floors stay gorgeous for years. |
| Hardwax oil finish. Lifetime warranty on lamination and finish, maintained. | Industry-leading, all-wood, lifetime peace of mind. |
| Request samples → | VIEW MORE → |

```
KEMBER INTERIORS
├── FLOORING                       AVAILABLE IN NEW ZEALAND
│   └── E3plank engineered flooring, American Northern Oak — curated colour selection
├── WALL + CEILING                 AVAILABLE IN NEW ZEALAND
│   └── American oak lining — New Zealand specification to be confirmed
└── BROADER KEMBER CAPABILITY      KEMBER CAPABILITY (not stocked in New Zealand)
    ├── Interior doors
    ├── Cabinetry
    └── Eplank · EcoKlik · KemLam laminate
```

- Two labels, always in the availability-label style: **AVAILABLE IN NEW ZEALAND** and **KEMBER CAPABILITY**. Capability is never presented as stock, never priced, never given a lead time.
- Categories are added as rows under the brand as the New Zealand offer grows. The system never needs a rebrand to add one.
- The "curated colour selection" is not named in this guide because the New Zealand selection is not yet confirmed. Product colour names (Naked Oak, Tawny Oak, Barn Oak, Pure Oak, Bronze Oak, Burnt Copper, Pale Oak, Pale Cream Oak) are Kember's and are written as Kember writes them once confirmed.

## 10. Document system

All Frevini archetypes apply unchanged (covers: image-led, editorial, technical, closing; pages: section opener, text, text + image, full image, technical/data, quote, closing; slides: title, divider, statement, text, text + image, full image, data, closing; posters and one-pagers). Kember adds:

| Archetype | Anatomy (D) |
|---|---|
| **Product page** | 5 + 7. Availability label, then H1 product name, then a key–value specification list (wear layer, core, thickness, widths, lengths, cut, finish); image bleeding three edges. One product per page. |
| **Engineering page** | Eyebrow, H2, the section device at the top of the text column (≤ 40 mm), specification list beneath, XL between blocks; optional engineering photograph in columns 7–12. |
| **Product slide** | Availability label, product name, three key–value pairs, image columns 7–12. |
| **Engineering slide** | Section device left (≤ 360 px), material photograph right, one sentence of proof. |
| **Posters** | Product launch (application image + product name), colour launch (material image + colour name), engineering proposition (section device + statement), specifier communication (architecture image + statement + CTA). All follow the poster grid; they differ only in the leading image and statement. |
| **Product collateral** | **Product sheet** (A4, product page anatomy, technical cover metadata); **colour card** (editorial cover, then a strict swatch grid of material photographs with names beneath in H3 and cut/finish in Technical); **technical datasheet** (technical cover, tables, section device, revision table); **sample information** (one-pager, sample sizes and how to request); **specification sheet** (technical, two-column key–value with 0.35 pt rules, written to be pasted into a specification). |

Not permitted, as Frevini: title bars, coloured header bands, bullet-heavy slides, more than one chart per slide, rounded cards, drop shadows, circular buttons, quote-mark glyphs.

## 11. Information design

Frevini §10 applies in full (tables, charts, diagrams, specifications, callouts, key numbers, quotations, captions, footnotes, references, metadata, revisions), with umber for green and Slate for Olive. Kember extends it:

| Content | Standard (D) |
|---|---|
| **Three-layer construction** | The section device (§7.2), always labelled with millimetres, always 4 : 11 : 4 for E3plank; other constructions use their own true proportions. |
| **Board cross-sections** | Section device with profile: tongue and groove drawn at 0.75 pt; micro-bevel if applicable; dimensions in Technical style. |
| **Dimensions** | Metric first, imperial in brackets; ranges with en-dash; "random lengths" stated as a range; widths listed ascending with a middle dot. |
| **Technical performance** | Table with property, value, test method or source; blank where Kember publishes no value — never a plausible figure. |
| **Installation** | Numbered steps in Body, one per line; conditions (subfloor, moisture, acclimatisation) in a two-column key–value list; warnings as a callout with the eyebrow "Note". |
| **Specifications** | Two-column key–value, keys in table-header style over 4 of 12 columns, 0.35 pt rules; written so a specifier can paste it. |
| **Colour ranges** | Strict grid of material photographs, square-cornered, evenly spaced; name in H3 beneath; cut and finish in Technical; availability label on the page, not per swatch. |
| **Product comparisons** | One table, products as columns, properties as rows, values only, no ticks or icons; a key number pair where two figures tell the story. |
| **Charts** | Series order: umber, Slate, Oak Mid, Oak Deep. Flat bars, direct labels, axis dropped, ≤ 4 series. |

> Product information shown in this guide demonstrates information hierarchy and layout. Current technical data must always be sourced from the applicable Kember technical documentation.

## 12. Governance — the Kember Test

Any "no" sends it back. Then hide the lock-up: is it still Kember, and not Frevini?

1. Does it use the approved typography hierarchy — Marcellus sets it, DM Sans carries it, Medium for emphasis?
2. Is whitespace structural rather than leftover — about forty percent open in editorial and presentation layouts, generous margins and grouping on technical pages?
3. Is the palette restrained — umber, Oak White and white, Slate at most once, no blue?
4. Is material or photography doing the visual work — oak in photograph or section?
5. Is the hierarchy immediately understandable — one eyebrow, one heading, one idea?
6. Have unnecessary boxes, shadows, gradients, rounded cards and circular buttons been removed?
7. Is the language specific rather than promotional — a number where a claim is made?
8. Is technical information treated with the same care as marketing content, and labelled available or capability?
9. Does it feel consistent with the Frevini design standard without being mistaken for Frevini?
10. If the Kember lock-up disappeared, would it still feel like a sophisticated architectural material brand?

### Issue checklist

- [ ] Marcellus and DM Sans embedded or the fallback stack applied
- [ ] Lock-up: correct colour, clear space of half the wordmark height, one per face, ≥ 30 mm / 130 px; no "kreative interiors"
- [ ] Colours match the tokens exactly (`#312011`, `#F6F1E9`, `#8A8F94`); no `#2E90D0`
- [ ] Grid margins, footer "· Kember Interiors", page numbers
- [ ] Availability labels present wherever a product is named; capability never presented as stock
- [ ] Section device once per face, ≤ 40 mm, labelled in millimetres
- [ ] Tables horizontal rules only; charts in series order
- [ ] Metric first; every technical figure traceable to a Kember document; nothing invented
- [ ] Technical-data disclaimer on datasheets and product sheets
- [ ] Copy: sentence case, no exclamation marks, no promotional adjectives

### Unresolved source conflicts — flagged, not resolved

1. **Manufacturing origin.** Company records place a facility at 246022 County Rd 16, Mono, Ontario (Dun & Bradstreet, Panjiva); a business listing gives Saint Charles, Michigan (ZoomInfo); import records show shipments from a supplier in Binh Duong, Vietnam, and a "Kember Home" site operates at kember.com.vn. No manufacturing-origin claim is made in New Zealand material until Kember confirms in writing. "American Northern Oak" describes the species and sourcing, not the place of manufacture.
2. **Total thickness.** E3plank is stated as 4 + 11 + 4 = 19 mm (¾ in). A retailer summary gives 15–19 mm across Kember lines; other lines (Eplank, EcoKlik) may be thinner. Only E3plank figures are used here.
3. **Widths, lengths and cuts** (102–254 mm; 610–3048 mm random; live, rift, rift-and-quartered) come from retailer listings, not Kember documents. Confirm against the 2024–2025 catalogue before use.
4. **Warranty term in New Zealand, certification body, emissions class, humidity tolerance, Janka hardness** — not published in accessible Kember documents; nothing is stated.
5. **Wall and ceiling lining specification** for New Zealand is not yet defined; the category is shown as available on the instruction of the brief, with "specification to be confirmed".
6. **New Zealand contact details** do not yet exist; placeholders are used.
7. **Site CSS, logo master and official PDFs** were not reachable from the authoring environment (network policy). Every A value above is "as indexed"; confirm against the documents themselves.
